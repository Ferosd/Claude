How It Works

You upload the photo set you already have. No reshoot, no second visit, no drone licence and nobody from our side goes to the property.

Yes. Rightmove, Zoopla, Funda and realestate.com.au all take video on a listing, and we have not found a portal that bans generated footage outright. What every one of them cares about, and what regulators care about, is that the video represents the property honestly. That is our own hard rule: we animate the rooms in your photographs and nothing more. No furniture that is not there, no damp painted out, no room widened, no view changed.
Yes, and we make it straightforward. Three things have landed recently. In the UK the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 replaced the old Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations on 6 April 2025, and treats a misleading listing as an unfair commercial practice. In the EU the AI Act's transparency duty for artificially generated visual content applies from 2 August 2026. In California, Assembly Bill 723 has required disclosure of digitally altered listing images, and access to the unaltered originals, since 1 January 2026. None of it blocks what we do, because we never change what the property looks like, but all of it expects a label. Every delivery comes with disclosure wording you can paste straight into the listing, and we keep your source photographs so you can produce the originals if anyone asks.
